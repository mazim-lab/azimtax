Top 5 Tax Deductions for Durham Region Small Businesses

Keep more of your hard-earned money by claiming what you're owed. A guide for Oshawa, Whitby, and Ajax entrepreneurs.

Running a small business in Durham Region comes with its share of costs—from commercial rent in Whitby to rising utility bills in Oshawa. The good news? The Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) allows you to deduct legitimate business expenses to lower your taxable income. The bad news? Many business owners leave money on the table simply because they don't know what to claim.

As we head into the thick of the 2026 tax season, here are the top 5 tax deductions that can significantly reduce your tax bill, tailored for local business owners.

1. Business-Use-of-Home Expenses

If your "headquarters" is your home office in Ajax or your basement in Pickering, you can claim a portion of your household expenses. This is one of the most common—yet often miscalculated—deductions.

What you can claim: You can deduct a portion of your heat, electricity, insurance, maintenance, and mortgage interest (or rent) based on the square footage of your dedicated workspace relative to your total home size.

Tip: To qualify, your home must be your principal place of business OR you must use the space exclusively to earn business income and meet clients regularly. Keep your utility bills organized!

2. Vehicle Expenses

Whether you're driving to meet a client in Clarington or picking up supplies in Oshawa, those kilometers add up. The CRA allows you to deduct vehicle expenses if you use your personal car for business.

Two methods:

  • Full Logbook: Track every trip (date, destination, purpose, km). You can then claim the business portion of gas, insurance, registration, repairs, and lease/depreciation costs.
  • Simplified Method: While less common for corporations, sole proprietors sometimes prefer a flat rate, but the CRA generally requires a detailed logbook to withstand an audit.

Warning: Driving from your home to your regular place of business is considered "personal use" and is NOT deductible.

3. Advertising and Marketing

Did you run Facebook ads targeting Durham Region residents? Print flyers for a local mail drop? Sponsor a local hockey team in Whitby? These are all fully deductible advertising expenses.

This category also includes website hosting, domain renewal fees, and payments to agencies or freelancers who manage your SEO or social media. In 2026, digital presence is key, and the CRA recognizes the cost of doing business online.

4. Professional Fees and Dues

You can deduct fees paid to professionals who help you run your business. This is where we come in! Fees paid to Azim Tax & Accounting for bookkeeping and tax preparation are 100% deductible.

Additionally, you can claim:

  • Legal fees incurred for business purposes.
  • Membership dues for professional organizations (e.g., local Chamber of Commerce).
  • Business licenses and permits required by the City of Oshawa or Town of Whitby.

5. Meals and Entertainment

Taking a prospective client out for lunch at a local Durham restaurant? You can generally claim 50% of the cost of food and beverages. This applies to client meetings or team lunches.

Exception: If you host a company-wide party (like a holiday party) accessible to all employees, you can claim 100% of the cost (up to 6 events per year). It's a great way to build morale and save on taxes simultaneously.

Don't Guess—Know Your Numbers

The key to maximizing these deductions is impeccable record-keeping. The CRA doesn't accept "estimates." You need receipts, logs, and a clean paper trail.
