Maximize Your 2025 Tax Refund: A Durham Region Guide to RRSPs and Last-Minute Tips

Published on February 18, 2026

With the tax season upon us, residents and small business owners across Durham Region—from the bustling streets of Oshawa to the quiet neighbourhoods of Whitby and Ajax—are looking for ways to maximize their tax refunds. While year-long planning is ideal, there are still powerful moves you can make before the deadline. At Azim Tax & Accounting, we want to ensure you're not leaving any money on the table. This guide focuses on the single most effective tool for last-minute tax savings: your Registered Retirement Savings Plan (RRSP).

The Power of the RRSP Contribution

Your RRSP is more than just a retirement fund; it's a direct line to reducing your taxable income for the previous year. Every dollar you contribute lowers your net income, which can result in a significant tax deduction and potentially a larger refund.

  • The Deadline is Key: For the 2025 tax year, you have until March 2, 2026, to contribute to your RRSP. This is a firm deadline, so it's crucial to act quickly.
  • How it Works: If your annual income is $60,000 and you contribute $5,000 to your RRSP, you will only be taxed on $55,000. This can lower your tax bill substantially and may even drop you into a lower tax bracket.
  • Find Your Contribution Limit: Your RRSP deduction limit can be found on your latest Notice of Assessment from the CRA. It's important not to over-contribute, as penalties can apply.

Gathering Your Slips: A Crucial Step

Before you can file, you need your documents in order. By now, you should have received most of your tax slips. Ensure you have collected all necessary T-slips (T4 for employment, T4A for self-employment, T5 for investment income, etc.). Missing a slip is a common reason for filing delays and potential reassessments later.

If you're a small business owner in Pickering or Bowmanville, this also means having your bookkeeping records finalized, including a clear summary of your revenue and expenses.

Don't Overlook Common Deductions and Credits

While the RRSP is a major player, don't forget about other common deductions and credits that can boost your refund. A quick review of your expenses from 2025 can uncover forgotten opportunities:

  • Medical Expenses: This includes more than just prescriptions. Think dental, vision care, physiotherapy, and even travel for medical treatment.
  • Charitable Donations: Gather the receipts for any donations made to registered charities in 2025.
  • Work-from-Home Expenses: If you were an eligible employee working from home, you could claim a portion of your home expenses. The CRA has both a temporary flat rate method and a detailed method.
  • Child Care Expenses: These expenses can be a significant deduction for the lower-income-earning spouse.

The Advantage of Professional Bookkeeping and Tax Prep

Juggling RRSP contributions, finding deductions, and meeting deadlines can be overwhelming. This is where professional help makes a difference. Clean, accurate bookkeeping is the foundation of a stress-free tax season. It ensures you have all the necessary figures at your fingertips and that you're claiming every eligible expense.

For business owners, a professional can also help structure your finances to maximize tax efficiency year-round, not just in the final weeks of tax season.

Conclusion

The clock is ticking, but there's still time to make a meaningful impact on your 2025 tax return. Prioritize your RRSP contribution before the March 2nd deadline and take a final look for any missed deductions.
