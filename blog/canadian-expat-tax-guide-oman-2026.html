Canadian Expat Tax Guide for Oman: 6 Smart Steps Before You Leave Canada in 2026

Oman is an increasingly attractive destination for Canadians working in engineering, energy, education, healthcare, and project management. The move can be exciting, but the tax side is where many people make avoidable mistakes. If you are planning to leave Canada for Oman in 2026, the most important tax work usually happens before you board the plane.

1. Do not assume a move to Oman automatically ends Canadian tax residency

One of the most common expat mistakes is thinking that leaving Canada is enough to become a non-resident for tax purposes. In reality, the CRA looks at the full picture. If you keep strong residential ties to Canada, such as a spouse or dependants staying behind, an available home, or provincial health coverage, your position may be weaker than you think. Review the ties you are cutting, the ties you are keeping, and the documents that support your move before you leave.

2. Prepare for the departure return and possible departure tax questions

The year you leave Canada usually requires a departure return if you are becoming a non-resident. That filing can include the date of departure and, in some cases, deemed disposition issues often called departure tax. Not every person moving to Oman will owe tax on departure, but not every asset is treated the same way either. If you hold non-registered investments, private company shares, or other capital property, review the implications before you go.

3. Review your TFSA, RRSP, and brokerage accounts before you move

Registered accounts often create confusion for Canadians moving to the Middle East. RRSPs can often remain in place, but TFSAs are where people get tripped up because new contributions after becoming a non-resident may trigger penalties. Non-registered accounts also deserve attention if they hold accrued gains or dividend-paying investments. Get clarity on what stays, what changes, and what should be frozen before moving to Oman.

4. If you are keeping Canadian real estate, set up the tax side properly from day one

Many Canadians moving abroad keep a home, condo, or rental property in Canada. That is often where expat tax compliance gets messy. If the property will be rented out, you may still have Canadian reporting obligations and non-resident withholding issues to manage. If you plan to keep property in Durham Region or elsewhere in Canada while living in Oman, make sure the bookkeeping, income tracking, and tax handling are set up properly before you leave.

5. Keep your income, payroll, and employer paperwork organized

Many Canadians heading to Oman are moving for work, and that means employment documents matter. Your contract, relocation letters, start date, housing details, travel records, and compensation structure can all help support the timeline behind your move. This is also a good time to review your final Canadian payroll, any bonus timing, stock compensation, or employer reimbursements so those amounts are handled properly.

6. Build a simple tax file before you leave, not after the first CRA letter arrives

Good expat tax planning does not need to be dramatic. In most cases, it means building one organized file before departure. Keep copies of your move date, visa or work permit documents, housing arrangement in Oman, travel records, bank updates, investment notes, and any decisions made about Canadian property or accounts. That preparation makes the first tax season abroad much smoother and leaves you with the facts ready if the CRA asks questions later.

A smoother move to Oman starts with cleaner Canadian tax planning

Moving to Oman can be a strong career or family decision, but your Canadian tax position should not be left to guesswork. The key questions are usually straightforward: are you truly becoming a non-resident, what needs to be filed in the year you leave, how should your accounts be handled, and what Canadian income sources continue after departure. Getting those answers right early can prevent costly filing mistakes and unnecessary CRA stress.
