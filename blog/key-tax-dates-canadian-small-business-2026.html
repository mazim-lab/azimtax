Key Tax Filing Dates for Canadian Small Businesses in 2026

Published on February 16, 2026

For small business owners in Durham Region, from Oshawa to Pickering, staying compliant with the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) is a year-round responsibility. Missing a key deadline can result in costly penalties and interest, impacting your cash flow and adding unnecessary stress. To help you stay organized, Azim Tax & Accounting has compiled a list of the most important tax deadlines for Canadian small businesses in 2026.

1. Payroll Remittances

If you have employees, you are required to remit payroll deductions (income tax, CPP, and EI) to the CRA. The frequency of these payments depends on your remitter type, which is based on your average monthly withholding amount.

  • New Employers & Small Remitters: Due by the 15th of the month following the month you paid your employees.
  • Quarterly Remitters: For eligible small businesses, remittances are due April 15, July 15, October 15, 2026, and January 15, 2027.

Late remittances can attract significant penalties, so it's crucial to pay on time.

2. T4, T4A, and T5 Slips

Deadline: February 28, 2026.

You must file T4 slips for your employees and T4A slips for any subcontractors by the last day of February. Similarly, if your corporation paid dividends, you must file T5 slips by this date. These slips report the respective incomes to the individuals and the CRA.

3. Personal Income Tax Filing (for Sole Proprietors and Partnerships)

Filing Deadline: June 15, 2026.

If you are a sole proprietor or in a partnership, your business income is reported on your personal T1 income tax return. While the deadline to file is June 15, be aware of the payment deadline.

Payment Deadline: April 30, 2026.

Even though you have until June to file, your tax payment is still due on April 30. To avoid interest charges, it's wise to estimate and pay your taxes by this earlier date.

4. Corporate Income Tax (T2) Return

Filing Deadline: 6 months after your fiscal year-end.

For incorporated businesses, the T2 return must be filed within six months of the corporation's fiscal year-end. For a business with a December 31 year-end, the deadline would be June 30, 2026.

Payment Deadline: 2 or 3 months after your fiscal year-end.

For most Canadian-controlled private corporations (CCPCs), the tax payment is due within three months of the fiscal year-end. For others, it's two months. Don't let the later filing deadline confuse you; the payment is due much sooner.

5. HST/GST Return and Payment

The filing and payment frequency for HST/GST depends on your annual revenue.

  • Annual Filers: Your return and payment are typically due three months after your fiscal year-end. For a December 31 year-end, this would be March 31, 2026.
  • Quarterly Filers: Due one month after the end of each fiscal quarter.
  • Monthly Filers: Due one month after the end of each month.

Conclusion

Mark these dates in your calendar! Staying organized is the first step to a stress-free tax season. Proper bookkeeping throughout the year makes meeting these deadlines much easier.
